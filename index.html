The Situation

Early December 1862. Confederate General Thomas Hindman is gathering troops in northwest Arkansas. Union commanders James Blunt and Francis Herron are struggling to determine Confederate intentions before the armies meet near Prairie Grove.

Intelligence gathering, intercepted messages, scouts, couriers, and civilian reports could influence the decisions made before the battle.
